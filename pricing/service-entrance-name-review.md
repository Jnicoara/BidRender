# Service Entrance — name review (2026-10-14, Track C)

**Read-only review. No name has been changed.** For each row on the "Service
Entrance" shelf of `starter-catalog-pricing.xlsx` ("Starter prices",
spreadsheet rows **955–964**, 10 rows, in the app-order sort of `28245633`),
the question is whether one real product can be priced at Platt or Home
Depot from the name alone. The workbook was read from a fresh copy, because
the owner has it open. It is the shelf after Wall Plates & Misc
(`wallplates-misc-name-review.md`); Panels start at row 965.

**The test** uses the properties a supply house names these parts by. For a
cap or a strap, the cable it fits (SE cable vs conduit; the conductor range).
For an elbow, the trade size and material. For a meter part, whether it is
the contractor's (a sealing ring) or the utility's (a tag seal). For a
bonding part, the panel family. A name that leaves out something that
changes the price is "vague".

**Verdicts:** **OK**; **Needs a word / words**, with the exact words;
**Not a real product**; **Two products in one row**; **Owner pick**;
**Decided**, where an earlier review already settled it.

**Used by:** no shipped starter and no run type uses any row on this shelf
(the sheet says "Not in an assembly" on all ten). The overhead service
starter PG2 prices its mast from `2" rigid conduit` and its head from
`2" metal weatherhead` on Conduit Fittings, not from these rows.

**Sources.** Listings checked on 2026-10-14 at homedepot.com, and where it
showed no price, another supply house is named (Crescent, Elliott, EMI,
Dixon, Stoneway, Lakeland). Prices only show how big a question is; they are
not proposed prices.

**Already decided, and not reopened here:**

- **The three service masts retire into rigid conduit** (owner, 2026-10-12
  and 2026-10-13; `conduit-name-review.md`): `Service mast, 2", 10 ft` →
  `2" rigid conduit`, and the 2-1/2" and 3" masts likewise, old names kept
  as search words. Watch the unit: the mast was `each` (one 10 ft stick),
  rigid is `foot`.
- **"Service entrance cap" is for SE cable, not a conduit weatherhead** (its
  description since the seed). The conduit heads are the `{size} metal
weatherhead` / `PVC weatherhead` rows on Conduit Fittings, and their
  clamp-on / threaded question is the Conduit review's Q4.
- **"Service entrance elbow" lost its "sill plate" search word** (catalog
  reality check, 2026-10-09), so it is the conduit elbow (SLB), not the SE
  cable sill plate.
- **Commodity items are generic; brands exist on panels and breakers only**
  (CLAUDE.md § Brands). The brands in "Why" are listings.

## Things that could price a bid wrong

None of these reaches a shipped starter today; all ten rows are "Not in an
assembly". They price a company's own service assembly, or a line added by
hand.

**P1 — the ground rod well is $11 or $100+, by what is typed.** An
irrigation valve box ($11.53) is the easy Home Depot price; a listed
inspection well is $100–$140. Q7.

**P2 — the SE elbow is $23 or $245, by size**, and the row has no size.
Q3.

**P3 — "Meter seal" is an $18 sealing ring or a utility tag worth pennies
that the contractor never buys.** Q5.

**P4 — "Service entrance cap" can be priced from Home Depot's "2 in. SE
Cap"**, which is a conduit weatherhead (it already has its own rows). The
two are close in price ($16.68 vs $17.75), so the risk is the wrong PART on
the bid, not the money. Q2.

## Owner questions, with suggested answers

**Q1. Do the missing words go in the NAME or the DESCRIPTION?**
Suggested: **names**, as for every shelf so far, each old name kept as a
search word on its row.

**Q2. The SE cable cap: which cable range?**
Suggested: **one row, `Service entrance cap, SE cable, 1/0–4/0`** — the
100–200A residential range. A smaller cap (#6–#2) is a new row if wanted.

**Q3. The SE elbow: which sizes?**
Suggested: **three rows, 1-1/4", 1-1/2" and 2"**, aluminum, gasketed
(`{size} service entrance elbow (SLB), aluminum`) — the 100–200A sizes. The
old name becomes a search word on the 2" row.

**Q4. Main bonding jumper kit: keep it, and how?**
Suggested: **keep it generic**, with the description "Usually ships with the
panel — add only when it is missing. Fits the panel's own family (QO,
Homeline, …)." Same treatment as the panel filler plate (Wall Plates Q6).

**Q5. "Meter seal": which product?**
Suggested: **the sealing ring.** Rename to `Meter sealing ring, ring-type
socket` (Square D 29008W class). The utility's tag seal is not the
contractor's part; "meter seal" stays a search word.

**Q6. "Meter socket adapter ring": retire it?**
Suggested: **yes, retire (hidden, never deleted).** Nothing uses it, and no
product is sold under that name. "meter ring" becomes a search word on the
sealing ring (Q5).

**Q7. Ground rod well: rename it?**
Suggested: **yes,** `Ground rod inspection well, 10", HDPE`; "access well"
and "test well" stay search words.

**Q8. Add M1 (SE cable sill plate) and M2 (SE cable strap)?**
Suggested: **yes to both.**
