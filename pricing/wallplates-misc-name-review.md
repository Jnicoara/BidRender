# Wall Plates & Misc — name review (2026-10-14, Track C)

**Read-only review. No name has been changed.** For each row on the "Wall
Plates & Misc" shelf of `starter-catalog-pricing.xlsx` ("Starter prices",
spreadsheet rows **862–954**, 93 rows, in the app-order sort of `28245633`),
the question is whether one real product can be priced at Platt or Home
Depot from the name alone. The workbook was read from a fresh copy, because
the owner has it open. It is the shelf after Switches
(`switches-name-review.md`); Service Entrance starts at row 955.

**The test** uses the properties a supply house names a plate or cover by:
gangs; the opening of each gang (toggle, duplex, decorator, single
receptacle, blank); material (nylon, stainless — and for stainless, the
grade); size (standard, midway, jumbo/oversized); screw or screwless. For a
weatherproof cover: flip (damp) or in-use (wet); extra-duty or not; plastic
or die-cast; gangs; opening; orientation; depth. For hardware: thread and
length. A name that leaves out something that changes the price is "vague".

**Verdicts:** **OK**; **Needs a word / words**, with the exact words;
**Not a real product**; **Two products in one row**; **Owner pick**.

**Used by:** shipped starters (`BASELINE_ASSEMBLIES` via `starterPartName`,
the same count as the sheet's "Used by"). Each starter's device was read
too, to check that the plate's opening matches it. **Every starter's plate
matches its device:** toggle plates on toggle switches; decorator plates on
GFCI, dimmers, sensors and USB; duplex on duplexes and the
switch/receptacle combo; single receptacle on the 20A singles. No run type
uses this shelf.

**Sources.** Listings checked on 2026-10-14 at homedepot.com and platt.com.
Where neither showed a price, another supply house is named (Elliott,
Gerrie, Sydist, Crescent, EMI, Standard Electric, SupplyHouse, Markertek,
Stoneway). Prices only show how big a question is; they are not proposed
prices.

**Already decided, and not reopened here:**

- **The cover family (owner, 2026-10-08):** one WHITE row per plate in nylon
  and in stainless, 1- to 4-gang, openings spelled out left to right
  (toggle, duplex, decorator, single receptacle); colours are search words,
  not rows; midway in nylon, oversized in stainless; one 30A/50A plate per
  material (`server/seed/materials/devices.ts`).
- **The catalog reality check, 2026-10-09 (owner-approved):** the unstocked
  mixed 3-gang plates and the stainless oversized duplex/decorator are
  retired; "N-gang blank plate" retired into `N-gang wall plate, blank,
nylon`; "Duplex/toggle combo plate" into `2-gang wall plate, toggle/duplex,
nylon`; "Stainless steel weatherproof cover" renamed to "Weatherproof flip
  cover, stainless"; the 30A/50A nylon plate gained "midway" as a search
  word (`shared/catalogRealityCheck20261009.ts`).
- **"Weatherproof in-use cover, 2-gang" is the in-use kind** (2026-09-25).
- **A weatherproof cover for a toggle switch is W2 of the Switches review**
  (row 861 renamed into it, Q9 answered 2026-10-13), not a new row here.
- **Commodity items are generic; brands exist on panels and breakers only**
  (CLAUDE.md § Brands). The brands in "Why" are listings.

## Things that could price a bid wrong

**P1 — every plate says `box of 25` / 25, and the screwless plate says
`box of 100`.** A 25-pack of standard white nylon 1-gang plates is a real
carton (HD sells 25-packs; distributors sell by 200). But stainless,
midway, oversized and 3- and 4-gang plates are listed **each** (Platt
Leviton 84103-40 $7.71 EA; Elliott SS8 $2.91 each), and the screwless plate
is sold singly or in 6- and 10-packs. A price typed from one of those
listings into a 25-pack row prices the plate at **1/25** of its cost. Q5.

**P2 — "stainless" is two grades, three times apart in price.** Type 430
(the cheap, magnetic one): Leviton 84003 **$1.01**. Type 302/304 (what a
commercial spec calls for): P&S SS8 **$2.91** (Elliott). No starter uses a
stainless plate today, so this is about the next bid, not the shipped
ones. Q3.

**P3 — the in-use covers on DV8, LT17, MH6 and PG20 are priced from a name
that does not say "extra-duty".** NEC 406.9(B)(1) requires an extra-duty
in-use cover on a 15/20A receptacle in a wet location. A non-extra-duty
bubble cover is cheaper and not legal there, and a die-cast one is three
times the plastic one ($10.48 vs $33.19). Q8.

**P4 — two rows for one plate.** The generic rows ("Wall plate", "2-gang
wall plate", "3-gang wall plate", "4-gang wall plate", "Stainless steel
wall plate", "Jumbo wall plate") sit beside the family rows that ARE those
plates. Priced from different listings, the same plate costs two amounts.
A company that picks the generic row by hand prices a plate with no opening
or material. No shipped starter uses them. Q2.

**P5 — the screwless plate is about ten times a standard one** ($12.22 vs
$0.43–$2.45 for the same Decora opening). Its name does not say decorator
or 1-gang, so it can be priced as a plain plate, or picked for a job that
meant a plain plate.

**P6 — Device shim says `each` / 1, but it is sold in 5s, 25s and 100s**
(HD $4.38 per 5, $13.37 per 25). A pack price typed into the `each` row
prices one shim at 25 times its cost. It is not a starter part. Belongs in
the pack-label scan (`pack-label-scan.md`, kind B).

**P7 — the panel filler plate fits one panel family** (QOFP $3.11, HOMFP
$6.35). DR4 prices it, `defaultQty` 4. Q6.
