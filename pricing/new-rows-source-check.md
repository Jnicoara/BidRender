## The owner's rule (2026-10-14) — for every name review, past and future

- **COMMON** — a part found at **Home Depot, Platt or Stoneway**: add the
  row; its **Example price comes from those three only**. Any other site is
  comparison, never the price.
- **SPECIALTY** — a part found at none of the three (fire alarm panels,
  sampling tubes, special breakers, …): **still add the row** so people can
  count it, but leave the **price BLANK (no Example price)**, with the
  description **"Specialty - price from your supplier."** The bid's "not
  priced" warning covers it (CLAUDE.md § 6 MONEY: a bid line nobody priced
  says "Not priced", never $0).

**This overrides** every "Listing" price in the earlier reviews' "Common
items that are MISSING" tables that came from another site (Rexel, Elliott,
EMI, Crescent, JRL, SupplyHouse, eBay and so on). Those figures stay in the
reviews as history and comparison; the Example price is the one below, read
at the three stores when A applies the row. Each earlier review now points
here (see "For A").

homedepot.com, platt.com and stoneway.com. A part counts as found only from
