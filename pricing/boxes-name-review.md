# Boxes — name review (2026-10-12, Track A)

**Read-only review. No name has been changed.** For each row on the "Boxes"
shelf of `starter-catalog-pricing.xlsx` ("Starter prices", rows 670–787 in
the app-order sort of `28245633`): can one real product be priced at Platt or
Home Depot from the name alone?

**The test** is the one a supply house names boxes by: shape and size, depth,
steel or plastic, gang count, mounting (nail-on, side bracket, bar hanger, old
work), cable entry (knockouts or clamps), and cubic inches. Fan boxes add a
weight rating; weatherproof boxes add hole count and size. A name that leaves
out a property that changes the price is "vague". A property that is the same
on every product of that type (a handy box is always steel) is not needed.

**Used by** = shipped starter assemblies that price from the row
(`BASELINE_ASSEMBLIES`, via `starterPartName`). A vague row used by many
starters matters most, because it prices all of them.

**Sources.** Listings checked 2026-10-12 at homedepot.com and platt.com, with
other supply houses or the maker's page where neither stocks the item (named
in the row). Prices are what the listing showed that day and are for sizing
the question only — they are not proposed prices.

**Renaming is not a text edit.** Any name adopted from this list goes through
`RENAMED_BASELINE_MATERIALS`, which renames the row in place, in the same
commit as the seed (CLAUDE.md § Materials). A row that becomes two rows needs
one of them added as a new material.
