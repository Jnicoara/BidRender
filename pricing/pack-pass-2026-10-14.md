# Pack pass — 2026-10-14 (Track A)

Owner's job: the pricing sheet never guesses a pack from words in a name
(`pack-label-scan.md` § E on `track-c`), and every pack decided in that scan
(A–D, E) and in C's shelf reviews is applied in ONE pass and one rebuild.

## What changed

- `packFor` (`pricing/starterSheetLayout.ts`) no longer reads words for a
  pack. A pack above one comes only from `STARTER_PACKS`
  (`server/seed/materials/starterPacks.ts`), keyed by exact name, each with
  its source. Nothing written → `each` / 1, or a per-foot LABEL at qty 1.
  `server/starterPacks.test.ts` goes red if a word rule comes back (shown:
  5 of 6 red with one rule put back). The old builder `writeWorkbook.cjs` lost its
  word rule too.
- Rows a store check could not settle are in `PACK_NOT_CHECKED`: they ship
  `each` / 1 and the sheet's Notes read "PACK NOT CHECKED — <what was seen>".
- Store check: Home Depot, Platt, Stoneway, 2026-10-14, by six read-only
  research passes. Platt prices nearly everything EACH (its "package" is
  the carton); Stoneway shows no price or pack without a login, so it
  settled nothing; HD pages were read through search listings (prices may
  be stale). The owner's precedent (MC connectors, C Q8) was applied: HD's
  contractor bulk pack wins over Platt's each; singles-only → each.
